Controls

  • WASD or arrows to move, C to walk
  • Space to jump, leap, or climb
  • On a ledge, W to climb, A and D to shimmy, S to let go
  • Space with A, D, or S to leap while hanging
  • E to rest at a shrine or take a scroll, R to drink
  • F to pick up or set down the cat
  • Shift for Ink Step once learned
  • J or left click to strike, K or right click to parry
  • P or Escape to pause
